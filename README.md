# HealthConnect Clinic Appointment Attendance & No-Show Analysis

## Project Overview

HealthConnect is a data analytics project focused on understanding appointment attendance and no-show patterns. The project explores how appointment characteristics, booking behaviour, previous no-show history, and reminder channels relate to attendance outcomes.

Completed as part of the AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship Programme, this project demonstrates a practical analytics workflow from data understanding and exploratory analysis to dashboard development, validation, refinement, and business recommendations.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse appointment attendance and no-show patterns.
- Develop and review key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Explore patterns across booking lead time, previous no-show history, reminder channels, and other appointment characteristics.
- Build Power BI visualisations to communicate findings.
- Test analytical outputs and refine dashboard usability.
- Translate observed patterns into actionable business recommendations.

- Appointment attendance and no-show rates

- Reminder channel patterns
- Appointment type and other appointment characteristics
- Waiting time patterns and appointment volume

## Key Findings

The analysis identified the following observed patterns:

1. No-show rates varied across booking lead time groups, with higher observed rates in longer booking lead categories.
2. Patients with previous no-show history generally showed higher subsequent observed no-show rates.
3. Examining booking lead time alongside previous no-show history provided additional segmentation insight.
4. Observed attendance patterns differed across reminder channels, with SMS recording the highest observed attendance rate among the categories examined.

These findings describe patterns in the available dataset. They do not establish that any particular factor directly causes an appointment to be missed or attended.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

- Review longer booking lead appointments for appropriate reminder and administrative follow-up.
- Consider previous no-show history when planning appropriate administrative attention.
- Monitor reminder channel performance over time.
- Use combined segmentation to support appointment-management decisions.
- Interpret rates alongside appointment volumes, particularly for smaller groups.


## Testing & Refinement

The testing and refinement phase included reviewing KPI calculations, checking important dashboard figures against the underlying data, testing filters and analytical matrices, reviewing visual clarity, and retesting important outputs after refinement.

The objective was to improve the consistency, interpretability, and usability of the final analytics output.

## Limitations

- Observed relationships do not establish causation.
- Segment volumes should be considered when interpreting rates.
- Reminder-channel differences do not prove that a particular channel causes better attendance.
- Findings may vary across datasets and time periods.
- Formal predictive model performance was not established.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates a practical data analytics workflow covering data understanding, exploratory analysis, KPI development, dashboard creation, testing, refinement, and business communication.

The final analytics output provides visibility into appointment attendance and no-show patterns to support appointment-management review and evidence-informed decision-making.
